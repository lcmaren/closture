# Closure Record  
## Decision Closure（Case Record）

---

## Context

This record documents the closure of an exploratory inquiry  
that began as a market analysis  
and terminated as a category definition.

The inquiry was not goal-driven.  
Closure emerged structurally.

---

## Initial Question (Implicit)

- Is there a non–labor-intensive market opportunity  
  accessible to a solo operator,  
  likely to expand over the next decade?

This question served only as an entry point.  
It did not survive the inquiry intact.

---

## Observed Constraints

### Constraint 1 — Usage-Based Interest  
It is repeatedly observed that **the practitioner does not sustain interest**  
in products he would not personally use.

This is not preference.  
It is a structural limitation of exploratory reach.

Branches that violate this condition do not unfold.

---

### Constraint 2 — Non-Simulatable Customer Cognition  
Customer-based deepening proved impossible because:

- The target users do not share Closture-style cognition  
- Their decision processes could not be internally simulated  
- The inquiry was future-oriented and non-empirical  

As a result,  
**no further branches could be extended from a customer-first frame**.

This path reached a dead end.

---

## Structural Shift

At this point, the inquiry pivoted:

- From *“Who is the customer?”*  
- To *“What structure does my own thinking require?”*

This was not a reframing for convenience.  
It was a return to the last expandable branch.

Market considerations were demoted from premise to byproduct.

---

- Closture (pre-existing thinking style)

**Decision Closure**

No further distinctions emerged after naming.
